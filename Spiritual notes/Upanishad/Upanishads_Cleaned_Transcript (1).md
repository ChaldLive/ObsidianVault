## Chapter 8: Taittiriya: The Five Koshas and the Map of the Self

Before the forest sages could merge the universe and the self, they realized they had to prove exactly where this silent
observer was hiding inside the human body. If the atman is inside of us, why can't we feel it? Why do we only feel our
anxiety, our hunger, and our stress? To answer this, they wrote the Taittiriya Upanishad. In this text, the sages
created a psychological map.

They explained that the human being is like a Russian nesting doll made up of five distinct layers or sheets called
koshas. We suffer because we falsely identify with the outer layers. The outermost layer is the physical body. Inside
that is the energy or the breath. Inside that is the lower mind, your raw emotions and reactions.

Inside that is the intellect, your logic and your ego. Most human beings spend their entire lives trapped in these outer
four layers. We say I am hungry identifying with the body. We say I am sad identifying with the emotions. We say I am
smart identifying with the intellect.

But the Taittiriya upanishad teaches that if you peel back all of these layers, if you quiet the body, slow the breath
and stop the thoughts, you reach the fifth and final layer, the bliss sheath, the atman. It is the absolute center of
your geometry. It is always there radiating peace, but it is muffled by the heavy noisy layers of the ego wrapped around
it. Knowing that this center exists is one thing, but how do we actually experience it? To explain the mechanics of
