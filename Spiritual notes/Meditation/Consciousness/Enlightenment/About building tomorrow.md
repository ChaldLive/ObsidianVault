**Bridge to the future, breaking the present school systems**

Find people who are successful in this world talkshow hosts, comedians, novelists, and attorneys. Put them in a room and ask them, "How many here got straight A’s throughout school?" None of them will raise their hand. By the way, throw in inventors, none of them are gonna raise their hand. Bill Gates dropped out of college. Michael Dell dropped out of college. The success of those people is not measured by the exam a professor wrote. Because they are thinking in ways you have yet to learn to think. They are inventing and manifesting tomorrow, and the only way they can do that is by breaking out of the enclosure that the present school system provides for them, by the exams written by people who are trained in another generation.



**Revised version**

Put the world’s successful people in a room — talk‑show hosts, comedians, novelists, attorneys, inventors. Ask them how many earned straight A’s in school. Almost none will raise a hand. Bill Gates dropped out of college. Michael Dell did too. Their success was never measured by exams written by professors from another generation. They succeeded because they learned to think differently — to invent, to manifest tomorrow. And they did it by breaking free from the enclosure of a school system designed to test memory, not imagination.
